This slim template includes a local MDX blog by default, so future projects can start with content files instead of rebuilding the blog architecture.

## Authorized reposts

For reposted articles, add these optional metadata fields to the article export:

- `source_url`
- `source_author`
- `permission_note`
- `canonical_url`

The public article route will render the attribution block automatically.
